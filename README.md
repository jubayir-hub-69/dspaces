# 🌌 dSpaces

**A modern, decentralized video conferencing and community hub for secure, low-latency, high-quality real-time conversations.**

## 🚀 What is dSpaces?
dSpaces is built for teams and communities that need reliable meetings without relying on a centralized provider. It offers real-time video and voice, Web3 sign-in, and an in-room AI assistant. Perfect for casual team calls or hosting large "Spaces" style webinars and AMAs.

## ✨ Core Features

* **🎙️ Important Meetings (Spaces Mode):** Host professional webinars or AMAs. Listeners join securely with muted mics and can use the "Raise Hand" feature to speak. Supreme Hosts and Co-Hosts have full stage management controls.
* **⚡ Instant Rooms:** Create a meeting in one click, or join with a Room ID. No plugins, no extra setup.
* **🤖 AI Assistant & Translation:** Capture live speech, ask questions during the call, and generate smart post-call summaries in multiple languages with Google Gemini.
* **🔐 Web3 & Email Authentication:** Login securely via Email OTP or connect your Solana Wallet for Web3-native identity.
* **🖥️ Screen Sharing & Grid Views:** Share your desktop while everyone stays visible in a clean, polished glassmorphism participant grid.
* **🎛️ Host Control Center:** Mute participants, remove disruptive members, and manage the room with granular permissions.
